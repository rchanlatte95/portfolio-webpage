SNAP GAP
TypeScript / React / Python / DuckDB / Gemini

Second-place prototype at DevFest Florida 2026 (DataKind Beacon track), built in 24 hours with Ketema Harris, each of us directing AI agents. One map for food banks and lenders: ask it a question in plain words and get a cited answer with a next step.

  • Mapped food access gaps across 490 census tracts in four Central Florida counties
  • Answered plain-language questions with Gemini, building every number from read-only SQL over a DuckDB warehouse
  • Showed a source and an as-of date for every number on screen, in the app and in AI answers
  • Put confidence, margins of error and missing data in plain language on every tract card
  • Fell back to built-in rules when the model was down, and said so on screen
  • Wrote requirements, contracts and tests first, so two people could build against one contract at once
